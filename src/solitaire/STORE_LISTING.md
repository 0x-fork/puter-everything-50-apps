# Solitaire: Klondike

**The classic card game, one card at a time**

## Short description

Play classic Klondike solitaire free in your browser. Turn one card at a
time, take back as many moves as you like, and finish to a shower of
bouncing cards. Add it to your home screen and it plays with no internet.

## Long description

This is the solitaire people mean when they just say solitaire. Seven
columns of cards, a face down pile you turn over one card at a time, and
four spaces at the top where each suit is built up from the ace to the king.
There is nothing to download and no account to make. Open the page and a
hand is already dealt.

Move a card by dragging it where you want it, or simply click it and the
game puts it wherever it is allowed to go. Changed your mind? Undo goes back
hundreds of moves, not just the last one, so one careless play never has to
cost you the game. If you close the game and come back later, the hand is
waiting exactly as you left it, though the list of moves you can take back
starts fresh from there.

On a laptop or a tablet the cards are drawn as real playing cards, with
proper artwork on the jacks, queens, and kings. Narrow the window to phone
size and the cards switch to a plain, easy to read number and suit in the
corner, so nothing ever gets too small to make out.

Win, and you get more than a message. Every card leaps off its pile and
bounces down the screen until the whole deck has tumbled away. If your phone
or computer is set to cut back on animation, the game respects that and goes
straight to the result instead.

Nothing here is timed. There is no clock ticking and no penalty for
thinking. The only number on screen while you play is your move count, and
when you win the game tells you whether you have beaten your own best. That
record is kept on the device you play on. If you happen to be signed in to
Puter, it quietly follows you to your other devices as well. You are never
asked to sign in, and nothing about the game changes if you would rather
not.

## Key features

- One card at a time, the way Klondike is meant to be dealt. No three card
  shortcut changing what you can actually reach.
- Take back hundreds of moves, not only your last one. (Returning to a hand
  you left earlier starts that list fresh.)
- Drag a card where you want it, or click it and let the game find the spot.
- Real playing card artwork on laptops and tablets, plain and legible cards
  on phones.
- A whole deck of bouncing cards when you win, or a simple win message
  instead if your device asks for less animation.
- Add it to your home screen or desktop and keep playing with no internet,
  once you have opened it online at least once.
- Games played, games won, and your lowest winning move count are kept on
  your device, and carried over to your other devices on their own if you
  are signed in to Puter.
- Free, and there is no sign up to play.

## Who it is for

- Anyone who wants plain, traditional solitaire rather than a version with
  its own house rules.
- Players who like to try a line and take it back if it does not work out.
- Phone players who are tired of cards too small to read.
- Anyone who wants a game that still works on a plane, on the train, or
  anywhere with no signal.
- Puter users who would like their record waiting on the next device without
  having to ask for it.

## What is new in this version

- Winning now sets the whole deck bouncing down the screen instead of
  showing a still message.
- The game can be added to your home screen or desktop and played with no
  internet.
- If your device is set to reduce animation, the bouncing is skipped and you
  get a plain win message instead.
