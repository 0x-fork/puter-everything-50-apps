# Backgammon

**Play the computer or a friend**

## Short description

Play backgammon free in your browser. Take on the computer, pass and play on
one device, or invite a friend to a private online game.

## Long description

Backgammon is the oldest board game still widely played, and this is it with
nothing in the way. There is no download, no account, and no setup for a local
game. Open it, throw for the start, and play.

Play whichever way suits the moment. Challenge the computer at Easy, Medium, or
Hard. Hand your phone or laptop across the table for pass and play. Or create a
private online game and send a friend the code, and you are playing as soon as
they join. Any game can carry a clock, from a one minute bullet game to a
fifteen minute one with increments, or no clock at all when you would rather
take your time.

The rules are enforced properly, including the ones that catch people out. You
must use as many dice as you can, and if only one of two can be played it has to
be the larger, so the board will never offer you a move that strands a die. Pick
up a checker and a ring is drawn exactly where it would come to rest. The
doubling cube is there when you want it and off when you do not, because a great
many people learned backgammon without one.

Online, neither player throws the dice. Each roll is agreed between the two
browsers before it lands, so nobody can choose their own numbers. Games against
the computer and in pass and play are saved as you go, down to the roll sitting
on the table, and any finished game can be replayed turn by turn and taken away
as a transcript.

## Key features

- Beat the computer at three difficulty levels, from a loose opponent to a
  search that weighs every reply you could throw back.
- Share one device for pass and play, or invite a friend to a private online
  game with a short code or a link.
- Roll dice online that neither side can choose, agreed between both browsers
  rather than generated by whoever is on the move.
- Switch the doubling cube on for gammons, backgammons, and a running score for
  the sitting, or leave it off for the simpler game.
- Race a clock with bullet, blitz, and rapid time controls and Fischer
  increments, or play with no limit at all.
- See every legal move before you commit, with the landing ring drawn where the
  checker will actually sit and pip counts for both sides.
- Pick any game back up after a reload, with the position, the dice, the cube,
  the move list, and both clocks intact.
- Replay a finished game turn by turn, jump to any position, then copy or
  download the whole thing.

## Who it is for

- Casual players who want a game of backgammon without signing up for anything.
- Two people sharing one screen, at home or on a trip.
- Friends in different places who want a private game together, and who care
  that the dice are honest.
- Players learning the full rules, including the obligation to use both dice
  and how the cube works.
- Anyone who wants a timed game, or wants to keep one and study it afterwards.

## Highlights in this version

- Online dice are agreed between the two players before either can see the
  result, so neither side can throw its own numbers.
- The doubling cube is a setting, off by default, and appears on the board only
  when it is in play.
- Undo puts a turn's dice back exactly as they were, so taking a move back is
  never a fresh roll.
- Any game copies or downloads as a `.mat` transcript that other backgammon
  software can read.
