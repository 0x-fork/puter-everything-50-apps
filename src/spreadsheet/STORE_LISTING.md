# Spreadsheet

**Tagline:** Smart sheets, files you own

## Short description

A fast spreadsheet with a built-in AI assistant, over 500 formulas, and clear charts. Your work saves as real .xlsx files in your own Puter cloud.

## Long description

Spreadsheet gives you everything you expect from a modern spreadsheet, plus an AI assistant that works right beside your data. Ask it to add a totals row, build a chart, sort a list, or explain what a formula does. It reads your sheet, makes the change, and shows each step it took, so you always know what changed. One click undoes a whole reply.

When you would rather do it yourself, the tools are ready. Over 500 functions cover math, statistics, lookups, dates, text, and finance, with autocomplete and plain-language hints as you type. Turn any range into a column, line, donut, or combo chart, then format your sheet with tables, filters, conditional formatting, and dropdowns.

Your files stay yours. Every spreadsheet saves automatically as a standard .xlsx file in your own Puter cloud, ready to open in Excel, Numbers, or LibreOffice. Version history keeps earlier snapshots, and unsaved edits are kept on your device if your connection drops.

Spreadsheet opens straight into a new sheet, with no sign-in needed to start. It works on desktop, tablet, and phone, in light or dark mode.

## Key features

- **Ask** the built-in AI assistant to add totals, write formulas, format cells, sort data, and build charts, with every step shown and one Undo for the whole reply.
- **Write** formulas faster with over 500 functions, autocomplete, and plain-language hints for what each one does.
- **Turn** any range into column, bar, line, pie, donut, combo, scatter, or waterfall charts that update as your numbers change.
- **Open** and save real .xlsx files that work in Excel, Numbers, and LibreOffice, and import CSV, TSV, and ODS files too.
- **Keep** your work in your own Puter cloud, saved automatically, with version history to bring back an earlier snapshot.
- **Start** in seconds from templates for budgets, expenses, invoices, loans, schedules, grade books, and more.
- **Organize** data with tables, filters, sorting, conditional formatting, dropdown lists, and pivot tables.
- **Work** anywhere on desktop, tablet, or phone, with menus, shortcuts, and a command search that keep every action close.
