# Point of Sale

**Your shop, in good order**

## Short description

Keep your shop moving with quick checkout, stock tracking, customer rewards, and clear sales reports. Built for independent shops, cafés, and market stalls.

## Long description

Spend less time managing the till and more time serving your customers. Point of Sale is a free alternative to Square POS, Shopify POS, Lightspeed Retail, and Clover for everyday checkout, stock tracking, and sales reporting. Built for independent shops, cafés, and market stalls, it brings your register, product catalog, and receipts together on your phone, tablet, or desktop.

Make each order easy to get right. Scan a barcode or tap a product, choose sizes and extras, add notes, and apply discounts. Hold an order while you serve someone else, then pick up where you left off. Record cash with automatic change calculation, log payments taken on an external card terminal, or split a sale across payment methods. Print receipts or share them as images.

Keep a closer eye on your business with low-stock alerts, stock counts, customer purchase history, loyalty rewards, and store credit. See sales trends and your strongest products, then explore gross profit and margins when you record product costs. Reconcile the cash drawer at closing and export records for your bookkeeping.

Your shop data is stored in your own Puter account, with automatic restore points and downloadable backups. After an online sign-in and initial load, a prepared device can reopen the register and record sales offline, then sync queued sales when the connection returns. History, catalog edits, and refunds require a connection. Card processing takes place on your separate payment terminal.

## Key features

- **Speed up checkout** with searchable product tiles, favorites, barcode scanning, held orders, and split payments.
- **Personalize every order** with sizes, extras, item notes, reusable discounts, and flexible tax settings.
- **Stay ahead of stock** with low-stock filters, stock counts, movement history, and product imports from CSV files.
- **Reward your regulars** with customer purchase history, stamp or spend-based loyalty, and store credit they can use at checkout.
- **Handle returns clearly** with full or partial refunds, separate refund receipts, and printable or shareable receipts.
- **Understand what earns more** with sales trends, top products, payment breakdowns, and gross profit and margins where costs are recorded.
- **Close the day confidently** by recording your opening float, cash movements, counted cash, and any difference from the expected balance.
- **Keep control of your records** with your own Puter storage, automatic restore points, full backups, and CSV exports.

## Who it's for

- Independent shops and boutiques managing a product catalog and stock.
- Cafés and takeaway counters with sizes, extras, and repeat customers.
- Market stalls and pop-up shops using a phone or tablet as a register.
- Owner-operated businesses that want checkout and daily reporting in one place.

## Current version highlights

Arrange your register around your best sellers, count stock with a review before changes are posted, and compare products by profit. Customer rewards, store credit, and automatic restore points help cover the work beyond checkout.
