# Checkers

**Play the computer or a friend**

## Short description

Play checkers free in your browser. Take on the computer at three levels,
pass and play on one device, or invite a friend to a private online game.

## Long description

Checkers is the classic board game with nothing in the way. There is no
download, no account, and no setup for a local game. Open it, pick an
opponent, and make your first move.

Play whichever way suits the moment. Challenge the computer at Easy, Medium,
or Hard. Hand your phone or laptop across the table for pass and play. Or
create a private online game, send a friend the code, and you are playing as
soon as they join. Any game can carry a clock, from a one minute bullet game
to fifteen minutes with an increment, or no clock at all when you would
rather take your time.

The rules are the ones you grew up with, enforced properly. Captures are
compulsory, jumps chain across the board, a crowned king ends the turn where
it stands, and a player with no legal move loses. The board shows you what is
legal before you commit: pick up a piece to see where it can go, and when a
capture is forced, the pieces it leaves frozen fade out so the rule is
visible rather than a surprise.

Nothing you play is lost. A game against the computer or in pass and play is
saved as you go, clocks included, so closing the tab or putting your phone
down costs you nothing. Afterwards you can step back through the whole game
move by move, jump straight to any position, and copy or download it as a PDN
file that other draughts software can read.

## Key features

- Beat the computer at three difficulty levels, from a gentle opponent to one
  that punishes a careless capture.
- Share one device for pass and play, with both players on the same board.
- Invite a friend to a private online game with a short code or a link, then
  resign or offer a rematch when the game turns.
- Race a clock with bullet, blitz, and rapid time controls that add seconds
  back with every move, or play with no limit at all. The clock only starts on
  the first move.
- Spot every legal move before you commit, including which pieces a
  compulsory capture leaves frozen.
- Resume any game after a reload, with the position, the captures, the move
  list, and both clocks intact.
- Replay a finished game move by move, then copy or download it as PDN.
- Read the board comfortably anywhere, from a phone to a desktop monitor, in
  light, dark, or your system theme.

## Who it is for

- Casual players who want a quick game of checkers without signing up.
- Two people sharing one screen, at home or on a trip.
- Friends in different places who want a private game together.
- Players learning the real rules, including compulsory capture and crowning.
- Anyone who wants a timed game, or to save and study one afterwards.

## Highlights in this version

- A warmer board and interface, drawn from the app's own icon palette.
- Compulsory captures now fade the pieces they leave with nothing to do, so
  the rule reads off the board.
- The move list folds away by default and opens when you want the notation,
  leaving more room for the board and the clocks.
- Games are copied or downloaded as PDN straight from the move panel.
