# Contacts

**Your address book, in sync**

## Short description

A fast, private address book that lives in your own cloud. Search every field instantly, build contacts from a paste, and import or export vCards.

## Long description

Contacts gives you one clean place for everyone you know. Open it and your whole address book is there: names, companies, phone numbers, emails, links, addresses, birthdays, notes, and tags. Start typing and the list narrows as you go, matching on any field, so finding the right person takes a second rather than a scroll.

Adding people is just as quick. Paste an email signature or the text from a business card and Smart Add turns it into a structured contact for you to check before saving. Star the people you reach for most, group the rest with tags, and jump between them with the keyboard.

Your contacts live in your own cloud account, not in a shared database, and they are never sold or shown to advertisers. Nothing to install and nothing to configure: sign in on any device and the same address book is waiting. When you want your data elsewhere, export a standard vCard file that Google Contacts, Apple Contacts, and Outlook already understand.

Contacts works in any modern browser, on a laptop, tablet, or phone, and adapts its layout to the screen you are on. It is free to use.

## Key features

- Search across names, companies, job titles, emails, phones, addresses, notes, and tags, with results narrowing as you type.
- Build a contact from a paste: Smart Add reads an email signature or business card and fills in the fields for you to review.
- Store the full picture: several emails, phones, links, and postal addresses per person, plus nickname, company, job title, birthday, photo, and notes.
- Star favorites and filter by tag to pull up a group of people in one click.
- Import and export standard .vcf vCard files, so moving in or out of the app is a single step.
- Select many contacts at once to export or delete them together, with undo if you change your mind.
- Move faster with keyboard shortcuts and right-click menus: press / to search, n for a new contact, and the arrow keys to move through the list.
- Switch between light and dark themes, following your system setting by default.

## Who it's for

- Freelancers and consultants keeping client details in one place
- Founders and small teams tracking partners, investors, and suppliers
- Anyone who wants a personal address book separate from a phone account
- People who value privacy and want their contacts in an account they control
- Anyone moving contacts between Google, Apple, and Outlook using vCard files

## What's new

- Multi-select mode for exporting or deleting several contacts at once, with undo.
- Tag rename and delete from the sidebar, applied across every contact that carries the tag.
- Offline support: the app shell loads even without a connection.
- Country-aware address fields, so postal details follow the format of the country you pick.
- Accessibility improvements throughout, including full keyboard navigation of the contact list and spoken result counts while searching.
- A refreshed app icon.
