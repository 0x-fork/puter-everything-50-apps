# Presentation

**Beautiful slides, made simple.**

## Short description

Create, edit, and present real .pptx presentations in your browser. Ask AI for help, present with speaker notes, and keep your work saved to the cloud.

## Long description

Presentation is a clean, focused slide editor for anyone who needs to make a good impression. Open a .pptx file or start from a polished template, then shape your story with text, shapes, tables, and images. The app opens straight into a new presentation, so you can start working before you sign in.

Your files stay real .pptx files from start to finish. Presentation edits them directly and keeps parts it doesn't edit, so you can share your work with anyone, whatever presentation app they use. When you're ready, save to your Puter cloud. Autosave takes it from there, and a local recovery draft protects your changes if your connection drops.

Need a hand? Ask the built-in AI assistant to draft slides, tighten your wording, build a table, or write speaker notes for every slide. You can see each change it makes and undo it in one step.

When it's time to present, the presenter view shows your notes, the next slide, and a timer. A laser pointer, keyboard and swipe navigation, and a quick black or white screen keep you in control in front of the room.

## Key features

- **Edit real .pptx files.** Open, edit, and download .pptx files without converting them, and keep the parts the editor doesn't touch.
- **Ask AI for help.** Draft slides, rewrite text, format objects, build tables, and write speaker notes. You can undo any change.
- **Present with confidence.** See your speaker notes, the next slide, and a timer, and use the laser pointer, keyboard or swipe navigation, and fullscreen.
- **Design with ease.** Add text, twenty shapes, lines and arrows, tables, and images. Objects snap into alignment as you move them.
- **Start from a template.** Choose a ready-made story or pitch deck in one of four color palettes, or begin with a blank slide.
- **Never lose your work.** Autosave to the cloud, keep local recovery drafts, and get a warning before a newer copy of a file is overwritten.
- **Work on any screen.** Edit and present on a desktop, tablet, or phone, in light or dark mode.
- **Share it your way.** Download a .pptx, print to PDF, or export your slides as PNG images.

## Who it's for

- Professionals who build pitch decks, reports, and team updates
- Students and teachers who prepare lectures, class projects, and talks
- Small business owners and freelancers who present to clients
- Anyone who gets .pptx files and needs to edit them quickly without installing software
- Speakers who want speaker notes and a timer in front of them

## What's new

- Laser pointer while presenting, in the colors set in the file
- N and P keys move to the next and previous slide
- Custom shows and slide ranges set in a file are followed when presenting
- Slides that advance automatically follow the timing set in the file, with playback controls
- Fonts embedded in a presentation display correctly, and you can embed your own TrueType or OpenType fonts
- Open and save files on your Puter drive with the system Open and Save dialogs
